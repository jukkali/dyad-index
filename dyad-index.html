Couples, parent–child, caregiver–patient, co-founders. Three figures, then the full evidence underneath.

The studies11 indexed
MDMA2020 · Open trial · 6 couples

MDMA-facilitated cognitive-behavioural conjoint therapy for PTSD

The foundational dyadic study. Both partners dosed; large effects on PTSD and relationship adjustment, no serious adverse events in either partner.

Monson, Wagner et al. · Eur J Psychotraumatol

Tier A
MDMA2021 · Secondary analysis

Relational and growth outcomes following couples therapy with MDMA

Growth, relational support and intimacy improved for both partners; behavioural accommodation and conflict fell — a property of the relationship that individual therapy cannot reach.

Wagner et al. · Front Psychiatry 12:702838

Tier A
MDMA2024–25 · Pilot · 8 veteran couples

MDMA-assisted brief conjoint therapy for PTSD

A brief-format replication reporting gains for both partners — with partial return of symptoms at follow-up, the most important caveat in this literature.

Protocol: Contemp Clin Trials Commun, 2024 · findings preliminary

Tier A
MDMA2021 · Theoretical

Couple therapy with MDMA — proposed pathways of action

Why a dyad might be the right unit: shared state, reduced defensive avoidance, and a jointly held reference experience both partners can return to.

Wagner · Front Psychiatry

Tier C
Psilocybin2017 · Qualitative · n = 20

Patients’ accounts of increased connectedness and acceptance

Disconnection to connection, avoidance to acceptance. Later operationalised as the Watts Connectedness Scale — but no partner was assessed.

Watts, Day, Krzanowski, Nutt & Carhart-Harris · J Humanist Psychol

Tier B
Psilocybin2017 · Qualitative · n = 13

Patient experiences of psilocybin-assisted psychotherapy

“Relational embeddedness” emerges as a core theme: forgiveness, encounters with parents and partners, and a strong need to tell the people close to them.

Belser, Agin-Liebes, Swift et al. · J Humanist Psychol

Tier B
Psilocybin2016 · Randomised · n = 51

Sustained decreases in depression and anxiety in life-threatening cancer

The evidentiary floor: the individual case is solid, which is exactly what makes the dyadic leap tempting.

Griffiths et al. · J Psychopharmacol

Tier B
Psilocybin2017 · Randomised · n = 32

Effect of psilocybin on empathy and moral decision-making

Emotional empathy up; cognitive empathy unchanged. Feeling more with your partner is not the same as understanding them more accurately.

Pokorny, Preller, Kometer, Dziobek & Vollenweider · Int J Neuropsychopharmacol

Tier C
Psilocybin2021 · Placebo-controlled · n = 191

Self-blinding citizen science to explore psychedelic microdosing

Microdose and placebo groups improved equally on every outcome. A shared microdosing ritual is a real relational practice — crediting the alkaloid rather than the ritual gets the story backwards.

Szigeti, Kartner, Blemings et al. · eLife 10:e62878

Tier D
PsilocybinOngoing · Recruiting

Psilocybin-assisted therapy for PTSD after intimate partner violence

Individual delivery, but it treats trauma that originated inside a dyad. Its safety findings will shape who can be admitted to conjoint work.

ClinicalTrials.gov · NCT06885996

Tier B
Psilocybin2024 · Review

Rationale for psychedelic-assisted psychotherapy in caregiver distress

The clearest published argument for a non-romantic dyad: caregiver and patient face interlocking demoralisation and role collapse.

Narrative review · PMC11658675

Tier C
Risks that exist only because there are two people6

Consent that isn’t free

One partner wants this; the other agrees to save the relationship. A reluctant partner is an exclusion, not an obstacle to manage.

Suggestibility

Psychedelics raise suggestibility. In a dyad the other person in the room has a stake in your insights, and their framing can be absorbed as revelation.

Asymmetric experience

One partner has a transformative session, the other a difficult or unremarkable one. Common, and absent from the literature.

The afterglow window

Warmth in the weeks after a session is real, and is not structural change. Agree in advance to defer irreversible decisions.

Separation as an outcome

Some couples emerge clear that the relationship should end. A programme marketing only repair isn’t equipped to hold that.

Contraindications travel

One partner’s risk disqualifies the pair. Dyadic work has a stricter admission profile than individual work, not a looser one.

Open questions this index tracks6

Does psilocybin work dyadically at all?

Its more internal, less verbally fluent profile may make it a worse conjoint agent than MDMA, not a better one.

Dose together, or separately?

Every protocol doses both partners at once. Sequential dosing — one partner sober and holding — has never been compared.

Does empathy without accuracy help or harm?

More closeness without more understanding could deepen a shared misreading in a conflicted couple.

How long do relational gains last?

The one follow-up signal available suggests the honeymoon question is real and unanswered.

Do non-romantic dyads respond alike?

Parent and adult child, caregiver and patient, co-founders. Only caregiver dyads have even a rationale paper.

What is the base rate of harm?

No dyadic study is powered to detect adverse relational outcomes, and retreats are not collecting them. The most fixable gap here.
